Istanbul Market District

The 16-place board, plus the card decks that start beside it. Unlock the layout to drag a deck wherever you want it — once a piece is dropped on a tile it stays fixed there. Set the number of merchants below, then move 1–2 places per turn from the Fountain. Player boards are larger. Use “Add player-board layer” to create square editable points; player-board layers can be moved, locked/unlocked, and removed. Sultan's Palace layers can also be resized. Palace cubes can be dropped onto layers.
